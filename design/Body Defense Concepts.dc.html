Turn 3

Bloodstream + an abstract body

2a's game, but the level list becomes a body you move around — nodes and lines, roughly body-shaped, no anatomy drawing. Illnesses change the rules rather than the enemy count, and vaccines are the one thing that survives a lost run.

3a

Where you are, what you're saving

Regions read as held, under attack, or not reached yet. The heart is the run — lose it and it's over, which is what makes a far-away forearm worth defending. Cases are different kinds of trouble: a cut bleeds you dry, flu multiplies mid-wave, food poisoning attacks your own cells, an allergy turns your immune response into the threat.

Turn 2

Three whole games

Each direction taken to a full loop — three screens: the moment of play, the thing you do between play, and the way you lose. 2a is Bloodstream alone, 2b is Lymph Lines alone, 2c is the hybrid where routing feeds the fight.

2a

Bloodstream — the whole game

A day cycle. Each morning something new is wrong somewhere in the body; you pick one problem, hold its waves, then sleep and the body heals a little. Classic, legible, easiest to learn. Risk: the meta layer is just a level list.
